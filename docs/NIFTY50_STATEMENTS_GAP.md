### Root causes
1. **Feed depth**: the original download scanned 30 pages of the NSE
   announcements API; for ~30 heavy-filing names it never reached FY22–FY23,
   so those filings were never *seen*, let alone downloaded.
2. **Refused PDFs**: NSE rate-limits; 6 names left with partial downloads.
3. **Audit tagging**: NSE headlines rarely say audited/unaudited and the feed
   has no structured field → 78% of records sit in class `unknown`.
   FIX APPLIED 2026-09-01: `_audit_from_pdf()` in
   `scripts/pull_annual_results.py` reads the Reg-33 header text
   ("(Unaudited)"/"Audited financial results") from the PDF itself when the
   headline is silent.

  quarters only).

  them, but unreadable scans may still yield nothing.
