The hidden problem

Different fund names. Same underlying stocks.

India's equity funds draw from the same deep pool of large companies. Three "different" schemes from three different fund houses can easily carry the same core bets — so your "well-diversified" portfolio may be far more concentrated than it looks on the app.

🎭

The illusion of variety

Six schemes across five apps feel diversified. But large-cap, flexi-cap and index funds often share the same top companies — sometimes the majority of their money.

🧮

Nobody adds it up for you

Every fund shows its own factsheet. Nobody shows what all your funds look like combined — because combining requires matching thousands of underlying holdings.

🔍

Your CAS already knows

Your Consolidated Account Statement lists every fund you own. Paired with official portfolio disclosures, it becomes an honest mirror of your money.

FAQ

Questions investors ask


What is mutual fund portfolio overlap?
Overlap happens when two or more of your mutual funds hold the same underlying stocks. For example, many large-cap and flexi-cap funds in India hold the same handful of companies, so owning several such schemes can quietly concentrate your money into fewer stocks than you think. An overlap check compares the actual holdings of each scheme you own and shows how similar they are.
How can I check if my mutual funds hold the same stocks?
Download your Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) from CAMS, KFintech, NSDL or CDSL — it lists every mutual fund you own in one document. Upload it to the Factsheet Engine AI investor X-ray, and it maps each fund you own to its officially disclosed monthly portfolio, then shows your combined asset mix, your true top holdings, and an overlap score between every pair of your schemes.
What is a CAS statement and how do I download it?
A Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) is a single document that lists all your mutual fund folios and demat holdings, issued monthly or half-yearly by CAMS, KFintech, NSDL or CDSL. You can request it free from the CAMS or KFintech websites using your email and PAN, and it arrives as a password-protected PDF (the password is your PAN).
Is it safe to upload my CAS statement here?
That is the core design principle. Your statement is opened in memory, parsed, and deleted immediately after your report is generated — it is never written to permanent storage, and your PAN (which unlocks the PDF) is never stored or logged. Your report is reachable only through an unguessable private link that expires automatically after 24 hours.
Does this tool tell me which funds to buy or sell?
No. It is a diagnostic tool for factual analysis only — it shows you verifiable facts about what your funds actually hold, where they overlap, and where you may be concentrated. It does not recommend buying or selling anything, and it is not investment advice. For decisions, consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser.
How much does the portfolio X-ray cost?
It is free for investors. The tool runs on the same holdings engine used daily by professional advisers, which aggregates over 250,000 holding rows across more than 50 asset managers from official monthly disclosures.
